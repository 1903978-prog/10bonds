# Credit research engine (Ollama worker)

How it works (owner request 1/11/2026):
1. **Queue (website, tiny):** every Top 10 answer calls `enqueueFromSearch` (lib/creditResearchDb.ts), not awaited. Bonds with a current assessment (under 30 days) are skipped; others get one job per ISIN in `app.credit_research_jobs` (P0 nothing stored, P1 stale). The same list is ignored for 5 minutes; an exposure is counted once per ISIN per 10 minutes.
2. **Worker (Lenovo):** `scripts/credit-worker/worker.mjs` claims jobs over HTTPS with a hashed, revocable token (`/api/credit-worker/*`), collects public sources (GLEIF, Wikidata/Wikipedia, the issuer's own site and PDFs, robots.txt and 2 s per host respected), and calls local Ollama only (temperature 0, JSON schema). Nothing heavy runs on the website.
3. **Scoring (website, arithmetic only):** `/complete` validates the facts (`lib/creditResearchSchema.ts`), `lib/creditScoring.ts` calculates IR and BR (version `ir-br-v0.1`), and one transaction archives the old row in `app.bond_credit_analysis_history` and replaces IR + BR together in `app.bond_credit_analysis`. `effective_grade` and exclusion columns are never touched, so portfolio selection does not change.
4. **Lists:** `gptStore` polls `/api/credit-ratings` every 20 s while a list is open and fills IR/BR when a job leaves the queue. `/api/credit-ratings?isins=` is public (IR/BR for everyone).

Fair scheduling: priority ages by 6 hours per level, so a P3 job outranks a new P0 after 18 hours. A dead worker's job is handed out again after the 15-minute lease. Failures retry after 5, 10, 20 ... minutes, five tries, then "failed" (Admin retry).

Limits of this first version: ratings are never "validated" automatically (only provisional or review_required); the model is not calibrated; only the Top 10 path and the queue exist (no EUR-shortlist expansion yet); retrieval finds only what an issuer's public site links.

## Publication gate (2/11/2026)

A finished job is not a publication. `lib/creditGate.ts` decides, on the server, whether IR and BR may be shown: `canPublishIR` and `canPublishBR` (separate), stored with every rating in `app.bond_credit_analysis.gate` and enforced again by the database trigger `app.credit_publish_guard`. Public readers (`/api/ir-br`, `/api/credit-ratings`) return only rows with `publish_ir` / `publish_br`.
States: researching, insufficient_evidence, review_required, publishable; `validated` only by a person (admin action `validate`). A validated or published rating is never replaced by a calculation that fails the gate; the attempt is stored in `last_attempt`.
Requirements (IR): verified issuer identity (LEI + register evidence) and type; at least 10 of 12 parameters (sovereign 9 of 11); all mandatory financial inputs of the issuer type, dated within 18 months (sovereign 24); 2 independent primary and 3 independent reliable sources (pages of one site count once; the site's own data copy counts as none); all six risk categories assessed with evidence; no unresolved conflict; range at most 15 points; reproducible calculation; and an IR built from the issuer's own factors (not yet true for model v0.1, so nothing publishes until the factor model is approved). BR adds: IR eligible, verified ISIN-issuer match, seniority, guarantee (explicit "none" counts), bond type and resolution treatment, callable and embedded risks, recorded issuer-to-bond adjustments, range at most 15, and never a bond-level agency grade minus a generic seniority penalty.
Corporate, sovereign, bank (and bank holding) and insurer have an approved method; other issuer types are blocked pending model approval. For banks and insurers every mandatory figure must be CITED and dated within 18 months (bank: CET1, total capital, NPE or NPL, ROE, cost/income, pre-tax profit, LCR, NSFR; insurer: Solvency II ratio, combined ratio).

## Method, evidence and reference cases (2/11/2026)

- Approved corporate model (`lib/livioIssuerModel.ts`, ported from the LM op model): IR from the owner's 14 factors and weights; unknown factors keep 0..100 bounds so missing data widens the range; BR = IR + capital-structure tier + structural flags. `estimated` and `rating-implied` factor values count as unknown; the agency grade is not an input. Banks, bank holdings and insurers: 7-factor model approved by the owner on 3/11/2026 (`lib/financialIssuerModel.ts`, weights and score tables in that file, draft in `docs/credit-bank-insurer-method-draft.md`). A factor made of several figures is known only if every figure is cited; judgment factors (business model, support, investments, reserves, business mix, capital quality) and the macro block are known only when a person typed them or a source states them, otherwise the range stays wide and the gate (width at most 15) refuses to publish. BR tier comes from the verified seniority. Other types (structured, supranational...) stay blocked.
- Evidence: the worker extracts figures deterministically (a sentence of the document must name the number) and sends the sentence as the citation; the website drops any metric without an accepted citation. NPE is never relabelled as NPL. Evidence counts as verified only if a cited figure or an official register record backs it.
- Retrieval: a reference case seeds the LEI and the official pages to read first; missing mandatory figures trigger targeted rounds (e.g. CET1 in capital/results documents), at most two rounds with a pause.
- Seniority: regulatory reference data plus final terms must agree to verify it; the site's data copy is a hint and a disagreement is a recorded conflict.
- Reference cases (`app.credit_reference_cases`): internal teaching examples (first: DE000LB2CW16, LBBW). Never public, never validated.
