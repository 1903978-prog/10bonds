# US Treasury benchmark (Federal Reserve + U.S. Treasury)

- Primary, 1-30 years: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, fitted nominal Treasury yield curve (FEDS 2006-28), `https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/yield-curve-tables/feds200628.csv`. Docs: `https://www.federalreserve.gov/data/nominal-yield-curve.htm`.
  - `SVENPY01..30` = fitted par yields (coupon-equivalent) -> benchmark `US_TSY`, curve type `PAR`. This is the default USD benchmark.
  - `SVENY01..30` = fitted zero-coupon yields (continuously compounded) -> `US_TSY`, `ZERO`. Kept apart (discounting / Z-spread later, after a convention conversion). Never interchanged with par yields or market YTMs.
  - Staff research product, not an official statistical release; may be delayed, revised or changed. Posted about weekly (Tuesday, data to the previous Friday), so on a Monday 09:00 Berlin run the newest day is normally ~10 days old. Status "current" = at most 12 days old, otherwise "stale"; the real date is always shown.
  - Format: ~9 note/legend lines, header row starting `Date`, then one row per day (`YYYY-MM-DD`), `NA` = no value (never filled). Percent units.
- Cross-check: U.S. Treasury Daily Treasury Par Yield Curve Rates, XML feed `.../interest-rates/pages/xml?data=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value_month=YYYYMM` (Atom/OData; `d:NEW_DATE`, `d:BC_<n>YEAR`). Only tenors 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 20, 30 years are used (months and `BC_30YEARDISPLAY` ignored) -> benchmark `US_TSY_TREASURY`, curve type `PAR_SELECTED`. Stored separately, never merged into or interpolated onto the Fed curve. Different methodology: differences to the Fed par yields are expected and shown, not corrected.
- Tables: the shared `app.benchmark_curve` / `app.benchmark_imports` (see `docs/bund-benchmark.md`), no new table. Unique per (benchmark, curve_type, observation_date, maturity_years); history is kept (each run saves the latest 10 complete days per curve, upsert, so it is idempotent); a failed or invalid download never touches saved rows; a move above 100 bp at one maturity versus the previous saved curve is refused.
- Code: `lib/usTreasury/` (core = parsing/validation/status, fetch, store), `scripts/ust-import.ts`, admin page `/admin/us-treasury-benchmark` (Par / Zero-coupon views, week change in bp, Refresh now, Fed vs Treasury table, Bund vs Treasury chart with currency and convention labels).
- Reads for other features: `getUsBenchmarkPoints()` ({asOf, points[{years, ytm}]}, Fed par), `getUsPar()`, `getUsZero()`, `getTreasurySelected()`. USD bonds use the USD curve, EUR bonds the Bund curve; never by issuer nationality. A yield difference vs the benchmark is labelled "yield difference versus government benchmark", not Z-spread or credit spread; EUR and USD levels are not comparable as returns without FX/hedging.
- Schedule: step in `.github/workflows/bund-curve.yml`, Monday 07:00 and 08:00 UTC, acts only at Monday 09:xx Europe/Berlin (DST-correct). Runs on a runner, never in the web process.
- Reuse terms (checked 28/10/2026): Federal Reserve (`federalreserve.gov/disclaimer.htm`): "Unless otherwise indicated, information on Board's website is in the public domain and may be copied and distributed without permission. Please cite to the Board as the source"; no warranty; Board seals/logos not usable. Attribution: "Source: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System". Treasury: U.S. government data feed, no restriction found on the feed pages; cite "U.S. Department of the Treasury". Status: internal use and admin display yes; public display is approved in principle with attribution but is NOT switched on (admin only) until Livio says so. No market data is sent by e-mail.
- Beyond 30 years: N/A, outside the verified curve.
